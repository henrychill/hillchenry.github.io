reflection:

As far as my professional portfolio is concerned, I think it’s going well. I’m happy with the content, and I’m excited to be able to add new components and projects as I complete them. It was also a goal of mine to have a publicly viewable resume website, and I think this portfolio has helped me accomplish and refine the final product of that goal. The way the website is set up was done from scratch, so I’m pretty happy with it, considering that. I think the setup could be improved, and a few viewing/functional bugs could be fixed. Yes, I think the pages are easy to read and navigate around, but I think they could look better if I used a different tool, but I wanted to learn and develop my CSS/HTML website development skills.

Through this process of developing my digital portfolio, I’ve learned key factors in presentation. Most notably, how to showcase my best work, present myself as a professional, find and communicate clarity in my career path and choices, and present confidence in myself and my experience as a student and professional. Resources that have helped me most are other people’s resume websites/online portfolios. Within this space there are hundreds of different pieces of information to include and ways to structure it, and finding what I prefer aesthetically and what is pertinent to include in the portfolio has been the largest challenge. But, looking at how others have set theirs up and what they included has helped me greatly.
