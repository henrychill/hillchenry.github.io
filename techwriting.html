1. Instructions/Documentation

How to Sail a Sailboat

Description:

This project is was a writing project to test my abilities in writing instructions for someone else to do a task/activity with only the reading material. I chose sailing a small boat with 2 sails. I chose this as my subject since I did this project the same semester I reconnected with my passion of sailing by joining the UCF Sailing Race Team, and as a capable sailor, I was teaching many new sailors the ropes, and I felt writing a piece like this would consolidate my thoughts and improve my ability to be a sailing instructor.

2. Resume/Cover Letter/Mini Interview

Application Materials

Description

Project #2 was designed to prepare me (in case I hadn't already) for how to apply for an internship or position within my field. We were asked to pick a company/position, and at the time I was very interested in Texas Instruments. As an IT professional, it was important to have a solid technical resume, a company-specific cover letter, and have sufficient interview practice. Despite having a resume, I re-wrote mine for this project, along with completing the other components from scratch, just for the sake of practice and familiarity with my own professional experience.

3. Research Project Proposal

Research Proposal

Description

Finally, towards the end of the semester, we were tasked with preparing a proposal for a theoretical academic research paper/project. My subject was surrounding the ethics (or lack thereof) of the current usage of our data, and how it feeds the information economy, and the possible issues that we face as modern digital consumers. I chose this subject since I am interested in data and cyber security on a personal and professional level. This project taught me how to source and cite academic sources, develop a plan of action for research methodology, and most importantly, formulate my ideas and create a technical hypothesis.
